# LinkedIn Post — PLA Part 2 Launch

The **Pragmatic Lakehouse Architecture** is built around resolving the tension between central control and domain autonomy — not by compromising between the two, but by deciding precisely where each one belongs.

**Part 2 — CDV Principle: A Deep Dive.**

**Converge → Diverge → Virtualize.** Three phases, one principle:

**Converge** — one classification at the source enforces itself through every zone that follows.

**Diverge** — domain teams own their data products. The contract is the handshake.

**Virtualize** — cross-domain questions get one governed answer. BI tools, AI agents, conversational interfaces, and APIs all read the same surface.

Three PLA Open Blueprints ship as working code: live federation across multi-platform estates, end-to-end Copper-to-Silver data contracts with both orchestrated and autonomous consumption patterns, and classification-driven security that propagates without per-zone reconfiguration.
